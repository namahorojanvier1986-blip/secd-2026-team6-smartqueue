### Competitor / Existing Solutions (M1 - 3 required)
- Qmatic. (2024). *Queue Management System*. Qmatic. https://www.qmatic.com - Does: ticket + display. Fails: expensive, needs hardware, no mobile pre-booking for SACCOs.
- Wavetec. (2024). *Queue Management*. Wavetec. https://www.wavetec.com - Does: virtual queuing. Fails: not designed for low-bandwidth / feature phones in Rwanda.
- Manual paper ticket / first-come-first-served at local SACCOs and bank branches, Kigali (Observed in interviews P1-P4). Does: simple. Fails: no order, no data, unfair, no priority for elderly.
