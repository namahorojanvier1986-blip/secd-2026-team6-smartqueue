**Team:** T6 | **Tag:** m1 | **Submission date:** 07/11/2026
**Full PDF:** `T6-M1-Discovery.pdf` in this folder

## Problem Statement (One Sentence)
Customers of banks and SACCOs who visit a branch to deposit, withdraw or apply for a loan struggle to use their time well while waiting, see their position, estimate their wait, or leave the hall without losing their turn because tickets are manual or missing, the order of service is unclear, and branches record no queue data. This causes waits of about 45 to 90 minutes at peak times, lost time for customers, stress, disputes and interruptions for staff.

## Evidence
E1-E5 published sources + E6: 5 interviews in Rwamagana (5 Nov 2026): 3 clients, 1 teller, 1 branch manager
